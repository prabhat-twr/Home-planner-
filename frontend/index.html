Scan a room. Get a measured plan
and its Vastu zones.

LiDAR meshes, ARCore depth or a single 360° photo go in. Out comes a metric floor plan with walls, doors, windows and furniture, each mapped to its compass direction.

The photo demo may take ~40 s the first time while a GPU starts up.

North heading is the compass bearing your phone faced at the start of the capture.
